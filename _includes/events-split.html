{%- comment -%} Sets `upcoming_events` (soonest first) and `past_events` (latest first) for the caller, using the events of the current language. "Upcoming" means dated today or later, evaluated when the site is built. Requires i18n.html to have been included first. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if lang == "en" -%} {%- assign language_events = site.events_en -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign language_events = site.events -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign today = site.time | date: "%Y-%m-%d" -%} {%- assign upcoming_events = "" | split: "" -%} {%- assign past_events = "" | split: "" -%} {%- assign sorted_events = language_events | sort: "date" -%} {%- for e in sorted_events -%} {%- assign d = e.date | date: "%Y-%m-%d" -%} {%- if d >= today -%} {%- assign upcoming_events = upcoming_events | push: e -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign past_events = past_events | push: e -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign past_events = past_events | reverse -%}
